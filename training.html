02 — Questions

Good to know.

Do I have to redo all of this every year?
No. Only the waiver is yearly. The Moodle orientation and each equipment module are done once, and in-person training is done once per type of machine.
Is the club the one training me?
No. Agility staff do all training and certification. The club runs events and can help you get started, but it can't sign you off on equipment.
Can I use the Zone for a class project or a side business?
Class work and personal projects are fine, and so is small-scale prototyping. The Zone can't be used for personal financial gain or high-volume production.
Can I bring my own materials or filament?
Sometimes. Personal materials have to be approved by Innovation Zone staff first, so ask before you bring them in.
Can I work in the Zone after 4:30 or on weekends?
Only with an Extended Hours Access card. Ask the Innovation Zone Coordinator about it.
I'm not a student or employee. Can I use the machines?
The Innovation Zone is only open to current U of L students and employees. Community members are welcome to join the club and come to our events.
03 — Zone rules

The short version.

Breaking a rule can lose you access to the Zone, for a while or for good.

  • Only use machines you've been trained and approved on.
  • Stay with any equipment you're running.
  • No weapons of any kind, including props and toys.
  • Personal tools and materials need staff approval.
  • Clean up and put everything back where it goes.
  • Don't waste or take raw materials or equipment.
  • Small-scale prototyping only. No personal financial gain.
  • No harassment, discrimination or bullying.
