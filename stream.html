STREAM — all of it
Process over product. What you learn while making something matters more than the thing you make.

Painters and programmers, poets and pre-meds: every discipline gets the same tools and the same welcome.

Seven letters

What each one can make.

S

Science

Build test rigs, sensors and lab gadgets to run your own experiments.

T

Technology

VR, electronics and code that talks to hardware.

R

Reading

Research the problem first: who is it for, and what already exists?

W

Writing

Pitches, documentation, zines and stories that sell an idea.

E

Engineering

Prototype parts on the 3D printers and laser cutters, then improve them.

A

Arts

Costumes, sculpture, vinyl, airbrush and anything that needs a maker's eye.

M

Mathematics

Parametric models, generative patterns and the geometry behind good design.
