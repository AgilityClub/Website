The STREAM Vision

Science, Technology, Reading, Writing, Engineering, Arts, Mathematics. We believe true innovation happens at the intersection of disciplines.

The Agility Club provides a student-led forum for innovation, entrepreneurship, and creative discovery. We support students through trans-disciplinary collaboration, creating the human framework and peer structure required to fully maximize the University's technical infrastructure.

What We Do

Tangible outcomes, practical opportunities, and hands-on learning experiences designed to keep pace with an evolving labour market.

Hackathons

We organize regular student-led hackathons. Collaborate with peers across faculties to solve real-world problems over intensive, high-energy weekends using Innovation Zone technology.

Pitch Competitions

Refine your entrepreneurial thinking. We provide the forum to practice pitching your startup ideas, securing feedback, and preparing for major events like Launch Point.

Industry Connections

We build direct connections between students, external partners, mentors, and the wider Southern Alberta innovation ecosystem to aid in commercialization and career growth.

Membership & Accountability

To maintain active membership status and privileges, general members must attend a minimum of two (2) Club events per month. Members failing to meet this standard will temporarily lose active status as per our constitutional bylaws.
