Explore · 01 · What we do

The workshop is open. Here's what happens inside.

The Innovation Zone has the tools. We bring the people, the structure and the reasons to show up.

  1. 01

    Hands-on workshops

    Beginner-friendly sessions on design, prototyping and new technology. You don't need any experience.

  2. 02

    Hackathons & pitch nights

    Form a team, build something in a weekend and pitch it. A good way to meet people outside your program.

  3. 03

    Chill Zones

    Relaxed socials with games, buttons, stickers and a first look at the newer tech. Come for the snacks, stay for the laser cutter.

  4. 04

    Student project teams

    Work on longer projects with other students, the way an incubator would, with the Zone's tools behind you.

  5. 05

    A student voice

    We bring student feedback to Agility so that its programs keep fitting what students actually need.

  6. 06

    Connections beyond campus

    Meet mentors, alumni and people from the Southern Alberta innovation community.
