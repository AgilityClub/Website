02 — Ways to collaborate

Pick what fits. We'll shape the rest together.

Sponsor

Fund a project, prize or event

Pay for materials for a student build, put up hackathon prizes, or sponsor an event. We report openly on how sponsorship money is spent.

Teach

Lead a workshop

Share what you know through a hands-on session built around design thinking and problem-solving: students solve a problem, they don't just assemble a kit.

Challenge

Bring a real-world problem

Give student teams a challenge from your organization, whether that's a community need, a design question or an early product idea, and see what they build.

Mentor

Mentor, judge or speak

Coach a team, judge a pitch night or give a short talk. An hour of your time can change where a student's career goes.

03 — How it works

Three steps, no red tape.

  1. Reach out

    Send a short email about your organization and what you have in mind. A rough idea is enough.

  2. Meet the team

    We'll set up a call or a visit to talk about goals, timing and what students would get out of it.

  3. Build it together

    We plan the event or project with you and handle the student side, including any ULSU approvals and Innovation Zone bookings.
